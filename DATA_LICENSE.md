# Data terms

Files in `data/inputs` come from the Mouse Phenome Database, GeneNetwork and published studies cited
in the manuscript, and keep the terms of their original sources. Files in `data/outputs` and
`supplementary` are derived from them; please cite the original sources when reusing the data.

## Diversity Outbred dietary-restriction data

`data/inputs/drido_bodyweights_survival.csv` is an extract of mouse identifiers, diet assignments, survival records, and body-weight measurements and their dates or ages from Di Francesco et al. (2024), [Data and code for Dietary restriction impacts health and lifespan of genetically diverse mice](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.24600255). The selected fields were combined into a CSV for this analysis. The source dataset is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## DrugAge

DrugAge Build 5 is provided by the Human Ageing Genomic Resources under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Supplementary Data 9 contains selected mouse records and source-based corrections. Cite Barardo et al. (2017), [The DrugAge database of aging-related drugs](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12585).

## Other published sources

The `elife-84060` workbooks are unmodified source files from [Garratt et al. (2022)](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.84060). The founder-strain survival medians are from Table S1 of [Yuan et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.13263).
