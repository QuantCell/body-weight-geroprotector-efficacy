# Data terms

The AGPL-3.0-or-later license applies to original code in this repository. Data obtained from the Mouse Phenome Database, GeneNetwork and cited publications retain their original terms and attribution requirements. Derived tables are provided for verification of the reported analyses.
## External olfactory-exposure data

The two `elife-84060` source workbooks are unmodified source data from Garratt et al. (2022), https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.84060. Their original publication and provider terms apply.


## Founder-strain survival data

`yuan2020_supplementary_tables.docx` is the unmodified supplementary table file from Yuan et al. (2020), https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.13263. Figure 3 uses the sex-specific founder medians in Table S1; the original publication terms apply.

## Semaglutide source data

`41586_2026_10940_MOESM6_ESM.xlsx` and `41586_2026_10940_MOESM11_ESM.xlsx` are unmodified source data from Feng et al. (2026), https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10940-7, published under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
